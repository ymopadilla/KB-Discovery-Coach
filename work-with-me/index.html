Frequently Asked Questions

What is Discovery Coaching?

Discovery Coaching is about getting curious about yourself. It's an opportunity to explore your thoughts, beliefs, emotions, and patterns to better understand what's keeping you stuck and what you truly want.

Through meaningful conversations and powerful questions, I help you discover new perspectives, possibilities, and choices.

How do I know if Discovery Coaching is right for me?

Do you feel stuck, unfulfilled, or unsure what you want next? Maybe life looks good on the outside, but something feels missing. Or perhaps you're tired of doing what you think you're supposed to do.

You don't need to be going through a major life transition to benefit from coaching. Sometimes you simply want to understand yourself better.

What happens during a coaching session?

We have a conversation! I listen, ask questions, and help you explore what's happening beneath the surface.

You won't receive a list of things you should do. Instead, we'll explore your thinking, challenge assumptions, and uncover possibilities you may not have considered.

The goal is for you to leave with greater awareness, clarity, and a sense of what you want to do next.

What is a One-on-One Discovery Intensive?

A Discovery Intensive is dedicated time for us to explore an area of your life where you're feeling stuck, uncertain, or ready for change.

Rather than rushing through a conversation, we create space to examine your thoughts, beliefs, and patterns, uncover new insights, and identify meaningful next steps.

Most Intensives run 2 to 3 hours, at $250 an hour. We'll discuss the format and what you hope to explore before scheduling.

Do I need to know what I want to work on before we meet?

Absolutely not!

Sometimes knowing that something doesn't feel right is enough to begin.

You don't need to arrive with all the answers. That's part of what we'll explore together.

Is Discovery Coaching the same as counseling or therapy?

No. Coaching focuses on self-discovery, personal growth, choices, and moving forward.

While our conversations may explore emotions and beliefs, coaching is not a substitute for mental health counseling, diagnosis, or treatment.

Do you only coach leaders?

Not at all!

Although I have spent years coaching and training leaders, my passion is helping people discover more about themselves.

Whether you're navigating change, questioning your direction, or simply feeling stuck, Discovery Coaching may be a good fit.

What if I can't afford coaching?

Please reach out. Each month I set aside a limited number of Discovery Scholarships at reduced or no cost.

Tell me a little about where you are on the scholarship page, and I'll be in touch personally.

How do I get started?

It starts with a conversation.

We'll talk about what you're experiencing, what you're hoping to discover, and whether working together feels like the right fit.
